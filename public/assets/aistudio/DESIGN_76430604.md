## Brand & Style
This design system captures an **Editorial Zine & Graphic Poster** aesthetic—blending the tactile, expressive charm of indie print publications with the structured clarity of modern grid-based editorial layouts. 

The personality is energetic, creative, grounded, and unapologetically tactile. It is tailored for creative technologists, multidisciplinary designers, and student leaders seeking to present technical rigor alongside artistic flair. Visual metaphors draw directly from printed paper ephemera: sharp ink dividing rules, layered die-cut app stickers, stippled watermarks, and vibrant yellow starburst accents that mimic retail price stickers and zine callouts.

## Colors
The palette evokes print inks applied to warm, unbleached poster paper:

- **Primary (`#8E0E2B`):** Deep burgundy/crimson ink used for expressive headlines, continuous structural rules, and primary calls to action.
- **Secondary (`#FBE94B`):** High-energy starburst yellow, accented with `#D9C41E` borders, used for badges, sticker bursts, and focal milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#EAE8E4`):** Soft stone/shaded paper panel tone for secondary section backgrounds and contrasting structural blocks.
- **Neutral (`#2B1117`):** Warm charcoal-burgundy for high-legibility continuous body copy and dark metadata.
- **Canvas Base (`#F4F3F1`):** Warm off-white newsprint tone serving as the default background, enriched with fine stipple or paper noise.

All text placed on `#FBE94B` must strictly render in `#8E0E2B` or `#2B1117` to guarantee high contrast.

## Typography
The system balances bold poster typography with clean editorial legibility:

- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in **Syne** (700–800) for structural character, uppercase confidence, and poster punch.
- **Body & Functional Labels:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans** (400, 500, 600, 700) for clarity, geometry, and high contrast against ink textures.
- **Handwritten Annotations:** Supplemented with script accents (e.g. *Caveat* 700) for playful margin notes, pointers, and sub-headings.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a **Print-Editorial Split Grid**:

- **Desktop (≥ 900px):** 2-column asymmetric split (38% left sidebar / 62% right content stack), divided by continuous `1.5px solid #8E0E2B` structural rules. The left sidebar remains sticky to showcase identity and contact coordinates while the right panel scrolls through modular stacked zones.
- **Below-the-Fold:** Expands into full-width editorial bands (projects, grids, and achievements) bordered by the continuous burgundy horizontal rule.
- **Mobile (< 900px):** Reflows into a single vertical stack. Border-right on the left column drops; sections stack cleanly with `1.5px solid #8E0E2B` dividers separating panels.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids generic blurred shadows, favoring physical print layering metaphors:

- **Die-Cut Stickers:** Raised physical paper effect achieved with a thick white stroke (`4px solid #FFFFFF`), subtle rotation (`-4deg` to `6deg`), and tactile offset shadows (`0 6px 14px rgba(43, 17, 23, 0.16)`).
- **Starburst Badges:** Overlapping layers resting above rule lines and panels, casting tight, warm contact shadows (`0 4px 12px rgba(43, 17, 23, 0.18)`).
- **Structural Rules:** 1.5px solid deep burgundy (`#8E0E2B`) rules acting as flat framing edges, grounding panels without box shadows.
- **Paper Grain & Halftone:** SVG noise grain (`feTurbulence`) overlay and dot-matrix halftone watermarks embedded directly into surface layers at low opacities (`0.03` to `0.14`).

## Shapes
The underlying shape language is crisp and architectural (`roundedness: 1` / `0.25rem`), reflecting cut poster board.

Exceptions are made for intentional tactile accents:
- **Pill Buttons & Tooltips:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full` / `999px`) for high-contrast sticker buttons.
- **Die-Cut Tool Stickers:** Moderately rounded corners (`14px` border radius) encased inside solid white borders.
- **Starburst Badges:** 14-point polygon clipping path (`polygon(...)`) mimicking jagged retail stickers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Sticker Pill Button (Primary):** Background `#FBE94B`, text `#8E0E2B` in bold display font, border `2px solid #FFFFFF`, box shadow `0 4px 10px rgba(43, 17, 23, 0.15)`. On hover, scale `1.05` and tilt `-2deg`.
- **Outline Action Button:** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #8E0E2B`, text `#8E0E2B`, uppercase tracking. Inverts to solid `#8E0E2B` with `#F4F3F1` text on hover.

### Starburst Badges
- 14-point clipped jagged badge in `#FBE94B` with `#D9C41E` border and dark crimson text. Rotated between `-12deg` and `6deg`. Used for milestone dates, categories, and sticker tags.

### Die-Cut Sticker Tray
- Tilted rounded tray (`rotate: -8deg`, background shaded gradient, subtle drop shadow). Overlapping white-bordered tool icons (`4px solid #FFFFFF`) with tooltip tags on hover that straighten and scale to `1.15`.

### Editorial Cards
- Background `#FFFFFF` or `#EAE8E4`, encased in crisp `1.5px solid #8E0E2B` rules. Top corners feature starburst tags or uppercase tracking metadata. Images feature monochrome defaults with color reveal transitions on hover.

### Annotation Callout Lines
- Thin `1.5px solid #8E0E2B` pointer lines connecting small uppercase labels (`11px`, tracking `0.08em`) to image corners and tray elements.

### Inputs & Text Fields
- Flat off-white background (`#FFFFFF`), surrounded by `1.5px solid #8E0E2B`, focus ring tinted with star yellow (`#FBE94B`), placeholder text in muted crimson (`rgba(142, 14, 43, 0.5)`).
